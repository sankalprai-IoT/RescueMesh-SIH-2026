# RescueMesh — Shelter Management

The shelter management layer supports shelter-level operations within the wider disaster-response workflow, including shelter coordination, resident assistance, capacity management, and operational support.

---

## Shelter Management Stages

### 1. Incident / Evacuation

Shelter operations can be initiated when an incident or evacuation requirement creates a need for shelter support.

### 2. Shelter Assignment

Evacuees are associated with an appropriate shelter as part of the response workflow.

### 3. Evacuee Intake

Shelter teams record and manage incoming evacuees.

### 4. Capacity & Occupancy

Shelter capacity and current occupancy are monitored to support operational planning.

### 5. Supplies & Shortages

Shelter resources, supplies, and shortages can be tracked for operational awareness.

### 6. Resident Assistance

Shelter teams support resident needs and assistance requests.

### 7. Shelter Response Coordination

Shelter personnel coordinate ongoing shelter operations and communicate operational requirements within the wider response workflow.

---

## Operational Boundary

Shelter operations remain connected to the wider disaster-response workflow while maintaining their own operational responsibilities.

Shelter capacity, occupancy, supplies, resident assistance, and shelter response activities are managed within the shelter operations layer.
