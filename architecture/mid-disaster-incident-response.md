# RescueMesh — Mid-Disaster Incident Response

The mid-disaster response layer handles emergency distress signals, incident processing, field coordination, verification, and controlled incident creation.

---

## Response Workflow

```text
        ▼

        ▼

        │

        │
        ▼

```
---

## Response Stages

### 1. Citizen SOS / Incident

Emergency information enters the response workflow through a citizen distress signal or incident report.

### 2. Area Resolution

The system determines the operational area associated with the incoming emergency request.

### 3. Command Review

The request is reviewed by the appropriate command authority before operational action proceeds.

### 4. Field Assignment

A suitable responder or operational unit is assigned for field verification.

### 5. Field Verification

The assigned field responder evaluates the reported situation and collects on-scene information.

### 6. 10-Field Verification Assessment

Structured verification information is recorded to support consistent assessment of the reported emergency.

### 7. Incident Conversion Gate

The verified information passes through an explicit incident-conversion gate before becoming an official incident.

### 8. Official Incident

Only an authorized and verified emergency can proceed into the official incident workflow.

### 9. Response Coordination

Once converted, the incident becomes available for coordinated operational response.

---

## Operational Boundary

An emergency SOS request does not automatically become an official incident.

Incident creation requires explicit command review and verified field information through the defined conversion workflow.
