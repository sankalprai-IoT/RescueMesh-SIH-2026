# RescueMesh — Operational Workflows

RescueMesh connects multiple operational stages into a coordinated disaster-response workflow.

## Incident Response

```text
Incident / SOS
      ↓
Area Resolution
      ↓
Command Review
      ↓
Field Assignment
      ↓
Field Verification
      ↓
Incident Conversion
      ↓
Official Incident
      ↓
Response Coordination
```
## Shelter Operations
```text
Incident / Evacuation
      ↓
Shelter Assignment
      ↓
Evacuee Intake
      ↓
Capacity & Occupancy
      ↓
Supplies & Shortages
      ↓
Resident Assistance
      ↓
Shelter Response Coordination
```
These workflows connect incident management, field operations, and shelter activities within the broader RescueMesh operational model.
